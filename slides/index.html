03

Question 1 of 4 · word for word as sent to jev.ai

Problem & User

“How clearly and meaningfully does the submission establish who has the problem, what the problem is, and why it matters?”

Extra rule sent with this question

“Direct user evidence is not required for a 5 on Problem & User. A submission that exceptionally clearly establishes the user, problem and consequences can score 5 even without direct user evidence.”

1

Weak. User and/or problem is absent, vague or difficult to understand. The submission may jump directly to an AI idea without establishing who needs it or why.

2

Limited. A user and problem can be identified, but the framing is superficial, generic or underspecified. Why the problem matters is weakly demonstrated.

3

Good. Clearly identifies the affected user(s), a plausible problem they face, and why the problem matters. The situation is sufficiently specific to understand what needs improvement.

4

Strong. Provides a specific and meaningful account of the user's situation, problem and consequences. The framing demonstrates strong appreciation of the user's context and makes the importance of solving the problem convincing.

5

Excellent. Presents a compelling, specific and meaningful problem. The user, their situation, what impedes them and the consequences are exceptionally clear, creating a convincing case that this is a problem worth solving.

04

Question 2 of 4 · word for word as sent to jev.ai

Problem Understanding

“How well does the submission demonstrate that the team investigated and understood the problem rather than simply assuming its initial idea was correct?”

Extra rule sent with this question

“Excellent user evidence alone can support a 5. Do not require both excellent user evidence and explicit root-cause analysis, given the limited time available to participants. Do not mechanically require techniques such as Five Whys, personas, journey maps or formal interviews.”

1

Weak. Understanding is mostly assumed. Little or no evidence or reasoning demonstrates that the team investigated the problem before pursuing its solution.

2

Limited. Shows some attempt to understand the problem—for example users, observations, assumptions or possible causes—but the resulting insight remains superficial or weakly supported.

3

Good. Demonstrates meaningful investigation through relevant evidence and/or cause-and-effect reasoning. The resulting understanding provides a reasonable basis for the proposed solution.

4

Strong. Demonstrates strong understanding through relevant evidence, thoughtful exploration of the problem, assumptions and/or underlying causes. Conclusions are well supported by what the team presents.

5

Excellent. Demonstrates strong, specific evidence that produces convincing insight into the problem. A single strong and highly relevant piece of evidence can be sufficient. Explicit root-cause analysis is not mandatory if the user/problem evidence itself demonstrates excellent understanding.

05

Question 3 of 4 · word for word as sent to jev.ai

Outcomes

“Does the team define success as a meaningful change for users or operations rather than merely producing an AI solution?”

Extra rule sent with this question

“A compelling outcome that is not explicitly measurable can score 4, but not 5.”

1

Weak. Success is essentially the production of an output—for example building an AI application, agent, chatbot or feature. Little indication is given of what becomes better for the user or operation.

2

Limited. Implies some benefit or improvement, but it remains vague, solution-centric or insufficiently connected to a meaningful user/operational change.

3

Good. Clearly describes the improvement the solution is intended to create for users or operations. Success goes beyond simply having built the solution.

4

Strong. Defines a specific and meaningful change in user or operational performance and gives a strong indication of how success could be recognised or assessed.

5

Excellent. Defines a meaningful user or operational outcome and an explicit measurable outcome or success measure showing how the team would know whether the intended change occurred.

06

Question 4 of 4 · word for word as sent to jev.ai

Test & Learn

“Does the submission demonstrate genuine experimentation and learning rather than assuming the proposed idea will work?”

Extra rule sent with this question

“Merely performing tests is insufficient for a 5. A score of 5 requires evidence that learning actually influenced the solution, a decision, or the next test.”

1

Weak. Assumes the idea or solution will work. No meaningful testing, learning, uncertainty or next-step reasoning is demonstrated.

2

Limited. Identifies assumptions, risks, limitations or possible tests, but demonstrates little actual learning or connection between these and subsequent decisions.

3

Good. Shows meaningful testing and/or examination of important assumptions and describes what was learned. There is some indication of remaining uncertainty or what should happen next.

4

Strong. Tests important assumptions or aspects of the solution, extracts meaningful learning, and clearly connects that learning to changes, decisions, remaining uncertainty or appropriate next steps.

5

Excellent. Demonstrates a clear test → learning → consequence loop. Evidence from testing produced meaningful learning that actually informed the solution, a decision, or what the team would test next.
